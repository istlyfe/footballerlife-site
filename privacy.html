Privacy Policy

Footballer Life · Last updated 10 October 2026

The short version

Footballer Life does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your careers

Your careers are saved on your iPhone and, if iCloud Drive is on, backed up automatically to your own iCloud Drive, which only you can access. We never receive them. If you use Back up, the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; it goes only where you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or Mail), under that service's own privacy terms.

Names you enter

Player and family names you type are fictional game content stored with your career on your device.

Purchases

The optional Footballer Life Pro unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment or personal details; the app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal data we can use to identify you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
