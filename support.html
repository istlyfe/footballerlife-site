Common questions

Where are my careers saved? On your iPhone, with automatic backups in your own iCloud Drive when it is on. You can also use Back up (career list, or Settings) to keep a copy in Files or iCloud Drive, and Import a backup on the career list to restore it.

How do I restore Footballer Life Pro? Profile › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

A week won't continue. Check Home for a decision waiting (an offer, a match moment or a family choice). If it still fails, email us; your save is never overwritten by a failed week.
